# Condensed model specification

The executable Python file in `code/` is the canonical model specification.

## Core constructs

- **Need:** monetary/exchange pressure generated by inflation, low conventional liquidity, and inequality.
- **Capability:** digital infrastructure, market breadth, regulatory capacity, trust, and local productive capacity.
- **Exchange islanding:** loss of external settlement/connectivity while non-zero local productive capacity remains.
- **Capture index:** concentration multiplied by one minus safeguards.
- **Abundance capture:** high productive abundance combined with restricted access due to concentrated control.

## Experimental design

- Synthetic jurisdictions are normalized scenario constructs; they are not empirical estimates for real countries.
- Architecture and policy comparisons use paired random seeds where applicable.
- The model evaluates mechanisms and failure conditions rather than forecasting dates, jurisdictions, exchange rates, or market sizes.
- Reported confidence intervals describe Monte Carlo variability conditional on the model assumptions, not population-sampling uncertainty.
